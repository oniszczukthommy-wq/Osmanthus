# 风格样曲来源与授权

每段样曲均为 24 秒试听节选，经过淡入淡出、音量调整及 MP3 重新编码；仅用于听感参考，不作为歌曲生成的音频输入。

- **chinese-pop**：心跳的声音 — Adeline Yeo (HP)。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/adeline-yeo-hp/single/xintiaodeshengyin/)。中文演唱，AI 人声。

- **heartfelt-ballad**：在你离开后 — Adeline Yeo (HP)。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/adeline-yeo-hp/single/zainilikaihou/)。中文抒情演唱参考。

- **urban-folk**：Clear Air — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100626)。原声器乐参考。

- **chinese-rnb**：Slow Jam — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600015)。R&B 器乐参考。

- **city-pop**：Chill Wave — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600048)。复古电子器乐参考，与 City Pop 有差异。

- **lofi**：Dream Catcher — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1800019)。Lo-fi 电子器乐参考。

- **dream-pop**：Dream Pop — HoliznaCC0。[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/only-in-the-milky-way-part-3/dream-pop/)。氛围器乐参考。

- **pop-rock**：Americana — HoliznaCC0。[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/rock-montage/americana-1/)。流行摇滚器乐参考。

- **indie-pop**：Paper Mate — HoliznaCC0。[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/washed-up/paper-mate/)。独立流行参考，含英文演唱。

- **chinese-style**：Shenyang — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600066)。东方传统器乐参考，现代编曲有所不同。

- **electronic-dance**：Dance Robot ACTIVATE — Loyalty Freak Music。[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)；[原曲来源](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Loyalty_Freak_Music/ROLLER_DISCO_DANCE_DANCE/Loyalty_Freak_Music_-_ROLLER_DISCO_DANCE_DANCE_-_02_Dance_Robot_ACTIVATE/)。电子舞曲器乐参考。

- **jazz-lounge**：Long Stroll — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100174)。爵士器乐参考。

- **cinematic**：Eternal Hope — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100238)。电影配乐器乐参考。

- **bright-acoustic**：Happy Alley — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100482)。原声器乐参考。

- **synth-pop**：Newer Wave — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN2000024)。复古合成器器乐参考。

- **gentle-piano**：Meditation Impromptu 01 — Kevin MacLeod。[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[原曲来源](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100163)。钢琴器乐参考。
